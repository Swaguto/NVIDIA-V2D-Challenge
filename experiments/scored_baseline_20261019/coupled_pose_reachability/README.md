# Coupled source-pose temporal reachability

Code-only archive of eight serial guarded CPU jobs, with 18 exact private
producer/guard/plan files and four executed repository dependency snapshots.
The manifest binds SHA values and private restore paths. Source data, candidate
banks, native models, private reports and commands are excluded from Git.

   actual URDF speed/position and original 0.25/0.05/0.05 rad bounds. Three
   analytical controls pass. This does not impose measured joint truth or
   qualify source/root/collision feasibility.
2. New ray-bound root geometry has 18 passing focused tests.
3. A finite thumb-configuration/ray-root/planar-finger bank has 58 eligible
   nodes. Empty rows are 109/110/112/114/116/117. Two geometric twist conventions
   and a finite grid do not cover all allowed poses.
4. New pair-state temporal reachability has 16 passing focused tests, including
   exhaustive independent small graphs and a nongreedy path.
5. First graph adapter fails before recurrence: a retained native thumb bound
   differs from the rounded URDF lower bound by 1.197e-9 rad. Its strict default
   1e-9 adapter rejection is retained.
6. The corrected adapter explicitly uses the ORIGINAL independent reference
   position-check tolerance of 1e-8 rad. No cap, source threshold or native gate
   changes. Bidirectional recurrence finds no complete finite path. Saved-error
   triangle bounds show the isolated middle-tip controls move at least 7.434
   and 3.882 px; the old exact-ray convention allows only 0.0001 px. The controls
   were imported, not regenerated or falsely claimed covered by that convention.
7. A genuinely new source-disk geometric projection uses the original image
   inequalities and reuses old eligible nodes without native rechecking. It
   produces 2,003 finite eligible nodes; rows 109/112/114 remain empty. At 109,
   generated roots/other-finger inverses fail before native evaluation. At 112
   and 114, all 140 and 1,155 newly evaluated full poses fail native self-contact.
8. The new disk bank's saved-record temporal graph also has no complete path.

This is a conditional finite study with 111/113 controls held as hypothesis
nodes, not a global reachability proof or selection of measured source truth.
The per-state rejected commands were not archived by the bank producers;
aggregate rejection counts do not substitute for independently replayable
per-state exclusion certificates. All worker logs, family failures and accepted
finite hypotheses remain private. Future interval studies must save their full
witness/certificate records instead of repeating these completed banks.

No source fit, old optimization family, native model compilation, physics step,
GPU/PPO or completed replay was run. Native morphology, effort, mass/friction,
source authority and all gates remain unchanged. The current three-component
freeze still has eight right-self and five hand/object failures. No full
reference, motor/grasp success, physical task, policy or score gain is qualified.
